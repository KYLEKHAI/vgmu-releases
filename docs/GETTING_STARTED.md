# Getting Started with vgmu

## Welcome!

vgmu is a mobile app for discovering, organizing, and enjoying video game music — online or fully offline. This guide walks through your first few minutes with the app.

## 1. First Launch

The first time you open vgmu, you'll see a one-time **Welcome screen** with the vgmu logo. Tap **Start Listening** to continue — this screen only appears once (unless you reinstall the app or clear its data).

## 2. The Four Tabs

vgmu has four tabs at the bottom of the screen, plus a Settings gear icon (⚙️) in the top-right corner of every screen.

| Tab           | What it's for                                                      |
| ------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **Home**      | Discover music — curated sections from the online catalog          |
| **Search**    | Find a specific album, soundtrack, or artist                       |
| **Library**   | Your personal collection — likes, playlists, folders, saved albums |
| **Downloads** | Track the progress of anything you're saving for offline listening |

Tap the **gear icon** on any screen to open **Settings**.

## 3. Explore Home

Home shows three sections pulled from the online catalog:

- **Latest** — recently added soundtracks
- **Top 40** — currently popular albums
- **Most Favorites** — all-time favorites

Pull down to refresh. Tap **Browse** in the header for more ways to discover music (see [Usage Guide → Browse](USAGE_GUIDE.md#browse)).

Tap any album card to open its album page.

## 4. Play Your First Track

On an album page, tap any track to start playing it instantly — no download required. A **mini-player** appears at the bottom of the screen showing the artwork, title, and playback controls. Tap the mini-player to open the **full player** with larger artwork, a seek bar, shuffle, repeat, and queue controls.

You'll need an internet connection to play a track this way. Playback continues if you lock your device or switch apps, and you can control it from your lock screen or notification shade.

## 5. Like a Track

Tap the **heart icon** on a track row, or the heart icon in the full player, to like it. Liked tracks automatically appear in **Library → Liked Tracks**.

## 6. Save Music for Offline Listening

Tap the **download icon** on a track or an entire album to save it to your device. Downloads are handled **one at a time** in a queue — you can watch progress on the **Downloads** tab, which also shows a live count badge of anything in progress.

Once a download finishes and is verified, it's added to **Library → Offline Library**, where it plays without any internet connection.

## 7. Build a Playlist

Go to **Library**, tap the **+ (Create)** button, and choose **Create Playlist**. Give it a name and, optionally, a description and cover photo. Add tracks to it from any album page, search result, or the full player's queue menu.

You can also create **Folders** to group playlists and saved albums together.

## 8. Adjust Settings

Tap the gear icon (⚙️) to open **Settings**, where you can:

- Change your **theme** — from a simple light/dark "Simple UI" with an accent color, to dozens of full fixed-palette presets (Dracula, Nord, Tokyo Night, Catppuccin, Gruvbox, and many more)
- Choose your **preferred download quality/format**
- Turn on **Wi-Fi-only** downloads and Home refreshes
- Connect **Discord Rich Presence** (optional — see [Usage Guide → Discord Rich Presence](USAGE_GUIDE.md#discord-rich-presence) for what it shares and its risks before enabling it)
- Check your library's **storage usage**
- Set your **default tab** on launch

See the full [Usage Guide](USAGE_GUIDE.md) for a complete tour of every setting and feature, or **Settings → Help** in the app itself for a quick in-app reference.
